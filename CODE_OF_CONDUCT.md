# Code of Conduct

This project follows a simple rule: contribute in a way that makes the work more useful, accurate, and trustworthy.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and specific.
- Critique ideas and implementation details, not people.
- Prefer evidence and clear reasoning over hype.
- Keep B2B trust and fact safety central to the project.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment or personal attacks.
- Publishing private customer, credential, or business information.
- Encouraging fabricated claims, fake certifications, fake customer cases, or misleading marketing language.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, close, or remove issues, comments, or pull requests that violate this code of conduct or move the project away from its fact-safety purpose.

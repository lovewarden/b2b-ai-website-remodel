# Before / After Demo

This fictional example shows how `b2b-ai-website-remodel` reframes a weak B2B website section into an AI-readable, buyer-decision-friendly structure.

## Before

```text
We are a professional manufacturer with high quality products and excellent service.
Our products are widely used in many industries. Contact us for more information.
```

## Problems

- Company identity is vague.
- Business role is not specific.
- Product category is unclear.
- No materials, specifications, process, or applications are visible.
- "High quality" is unsupported.
- Buyer does not know what information to send for an inquiry.
- AI cannot confidently summarize supplier suitability.

## After

```text
Example Precision Parts is a CNC machining supplier based in Shenzhen, China, providing custom aluminum and stainless steel components for automation equipment and industrial machinery buyers.

Product facts:
- Process: CNC milling and turning
- Materials: aluminum alloy and stainless steel
- Buyer input: drawings, material requirements, tolerance requirements, surface treatment, and quantity
- Applications: automation equipment, industrial fixtures, and machinery assemblies

Quality and trust:
- Add inspection process details when verified
- Add certificate information only when certificate scope is confirmed
- Add case studies only with approved customer or anonymized project evidence

Inquiry path:
- Send drawings and target quantity through the contact form
- Ask for material, tolerance, and lead-time confirmation before production
```

## What Changed

| Area | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Entity | "professional manufacturer" | company role, location, product focus |
| Product facts | absent | process, materials, applications, buyer inputs |
| Trust | unsupported "high quality" | evidence requests and safe trust structure |
| Buyer decision | unclear | what to send and how to inquire |
| AI readability | ambiguous | summarizable without guessing |

## AI Restatement Test

```text
Example Precision Parts is a CNC machining supplier in Shenzhen, China. It provides custom aluminum and stainless steel components for automation equipment and industrial machinery buyers. Buyers can evaluate it using verified inspection details, certificate scope, and approved case evidence when provided. The next step is to send drawings and target quantity through the contact form.
```

Do not use this demo as public copy for a real company. Replace all facts with owner-verified information.

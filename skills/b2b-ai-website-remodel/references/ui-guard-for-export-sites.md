# UI Guard For Export Sites

AI readability improvements should not make the website ugly, cramped, or generic.

## Visual Principles

- Preserve the existing brand identity unless asked to redesign.
- Use restrained B2B industrial style: clean sections, clear hierarchy, real product/factory imagery, practical CTAs.
- Avoid flashy SaaS design, decorative gradients, huge empty hero sections, unrelated illustrations, and generic stock photos.
- Make product categories, capabilities, certifications, applications, and inquiry actions scannable.
- Keep text blocks short and structured with tables, cards, lists, FAQs, and comparison blocks where appropriate.

## First Viewport

The homepage first viewport should show:

- What the company manufactures.
- Buyer or application fit.
- One primary CTA.
- Product/factory visual when available.

Avoid slogans that do not identify the company or product.

## Mobile Checks

Check:

- Long product names do not overflow.
- Tables scroll or stack cleanly.
- Header and nav remain usable.
- CTA buttons are visible and not cramped.
- Product images keep stable aspect ratios.
- FAQ and specification sections are readable.

## Procurement UI Patterns

Good sections:

- Product category grid.
- Specification table.
- Customization options.
- Quality control timeline.
- Certification strip with verified labels.
- Application cards.
- Inquiry checklist.
- FAQ accordion.

Use cards only for individual repeated items. Do not nest cards inside cards.

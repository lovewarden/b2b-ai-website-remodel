# Product Fact Framework

Use this framework to separate factual product information from generic marketing language.

## Goal

Product and category pages should tell buyers and AI systems what the company actually provides.

The page should answer:

- What is the product or service?
- What specifications or variants exist?
- What materials, processes, or technologies are involved?
- What applications and industries use it?
- What customization capability is verified?
- What does the buyer need to know before inquiry?

## Product Fact Fields

Check for:

- Product name.
- Product category.
- Specifications, dimensions, capacity, tolerance, finish, size, model, or variant.
- Materials or components.
- Manufacturing process or service process.
- Applications and industry usage.
- Customization capability, OEM/ODM, drawings, samples, packaging, private label, or configuration support when verified.
- Quality, inspection, or testing notes.
- MOQ, lead time, warranty, price, availability, or logistics only when verified and publishable.
- Inquiry CTA.

## Marketing Vs Fact

Weak marketing:

```text
High quality products with competitive price and excellent service.
```

Fact-based:

```text
304 stainless steel vacuum bottle with OEM logo customization, double-wall insulation, and corporate gift packaging options.
```

Only publish the fact-based version when those details are supported.

## Recommended Page Structure

Use factual sections:

```text
Product Overview
Specifications
Materials And Options
Manufacturing Or Service Process
Applications
Customization
Quality And Testing
Packaging Or Delivery
FAQ
Request A Quote
```

## Product Fact Gap Output

Use this format:

```text
Product Fact Gap:
Missing: Product specification table.
Why it matters: AI and buyers cannot compare whether the product matches their requirement.
Recommended section: Specifications.
Owner input needed: Confirm materials, dimensions, capacity, model options, and customization limits.
```

## Rules

- Do not recommend empty adjectives as improvements.
- Do not convert unverified capabilities into public copy.
- Prefer tables, direct answer blocks, short paragraphs, and procurement FAQs.
- If facts are missing, leave a clear owner question instead of inventing copy.

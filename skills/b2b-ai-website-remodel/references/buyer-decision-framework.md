# Buyer Decision Framework

Use this framework to analyze the website from a B2B buyer perspective.

## Goal

The website should reduce uncertainty before inquiry. A buyer should know whether the company is relevant, capable, credible, and easy to contact.

## Core Buyer Questions

Check whether the website answers:

- Who are you?
- What exactly do you make or provide?
- Can you manufacture, supply, customize, or service my requirement?
- What specifications, materials, applications, or industries do you support?
- Why should I trust you?
- What proof do you have?
- What is the inquiry path?
- How can I contact you?

## Buyer Types

Identify the likely buyer when possible:

- Procurement manager.
- Distributor or wholesaler.
- Brand owner.
- Engineering or technical buyer.
- Factory sourcing team.
- Project contractor.
- Corporate gift or customization buyer.
- Operations or maintenance buyer.

Different buyers need different decision information. Do not assume one generic buyer.

## Journey Checks

Homepage:

- Entity and product focus are clear in the first meaningful view.
- Buyer can identify relevant categories.
- Primary CTA is visible.

Category/Product:

- Facts, specs, applications, customization, and quality notes are easy to compare.
- CTA appears near decision points.

About/Capability:

- Capability and trust are supported with evidence.
- Claims are not purely slogan-based.

Contact:

- Phone, email, form, address, WhatsApp, inquiry form, or other verified contact paths are visible.
- For custom work, buyer knows what information to send.

FAQ:

- Answers common procurement questions: MOQ, samples, customization, lead time, quality control, certificates, packaging, delivery, warranty, drawings, and payment terms when verified.

## Decision Gap Output

Use this format:

```text
Buyer Decision Gap:
Question unanswered: Can this company customize the product for my requirement?
Why it matters: A buyer with custom specifications may leave before contacting.
Recommended change: Add a customization capability section and FAQ.
Owner input needed: Confirm customization options, sample process, drawings accepted, MOQ, and lead time.
```

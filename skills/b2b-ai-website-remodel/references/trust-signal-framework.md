# Trust Signal Framework

Use this framework to evaluate whether a B2B website gives buyers and AI systems enough decision evidence.

Trust signals are not decorations. They are procurement decision signals that reduce uncertainty and help buyers decide whether to contact the company.

## Quality Signals

Examples:

- Certifications and standards.
- Test reports.
- Inspection steps.
- QC process.
- Material traceability.
- Defect handling.
- Compliance documentation.

Evidence needed:

- Certificate names and exact scope.
- Photos, reports, process descriptions, or owner confirmation.
- Avoid publishing certificate logos or standards without proof.

## Capability Signals

Examples:

- Equipment and production process.
- OEM/ODM support.
- Customization workflow.
- Engineering or design support.
- Capacity and lead time.
- Material/process range.

Evidence needed:

- Equipment list, factory photos, process steps, production-line details, or confirmed capacity.
- If not verified, recommend a "Manufacturing Capability" or "Customization Process" section rather than inventing details.

## Experience Signals

Examples:

- Industries served.
- Application examples.
- Case studies.
- Project types.
- Common buyer problems solved.
- Export markets when verified.

Evidence needed:

- Case descriptions, approved photos, anonymized examples, or industry references.
- Named customers require explicit approval.

## Reliability Signals

Examples:

- Years in business.
- Company history.
- Response process.
- Sample policy.
- Warranty or after-sales support.
- Export documentation.
- Contact reliability.
- Delivery capability when verified.

Evidence needed:

- Timeline, milestones, policies, sales process, contact details, or service commitments.

## Gap Output Pattern

Use this pattern:

```text
Trust Signal Gap:
Missing: Manufacturing capability proof.
Why it matters: Buyers and AI systems cannot determine whether the supplier can handle custom production.
Recommended section: Manufacturing Capability.
Evidence needed: Equipment list, process photos, verified production steps, and customization workflow.
```

## Why Signal Matters Pattern

For each signal, explain buyer relevance:

```text
Signal: Quality inspection process.
Why it matters: A procurement buyer needs to know whether the supplier can control consistency before placing a custom or repeat order.
Evidence needed: Inspection steps, testing equipment, QC photos, standards, or documented process.
```

## Priority Order

Prioritize trust signals that affect buyer contact decisions:

1. Can this business provide the needed product or service?
2. Can it meet the buyer's specification, quality, and customization needs?
3. Is there enough evidence to trust the claim?
4. Is the inquiry path clear?
5. Can AI summarize the business without unsupported assumptions?

# Manufacturing Buyer Intent

Foreign B2B buyers are not looking for inspirational copy first. They need to know whether the supplier can make the product, meet the standard, customize it, ship it, and respond reliably.

## Universal Buyer Questions

- What exactly do you manufacture?
- Are you a factory, supplier, trader, or brand?
- What materials, sizes, models, grades, or specifications are available?
- Can you customize by drawing, sample, formula, logo, packaging, mold, or OEM/ODM requirement?
- What certifications, standards, or test reports are available?
- What is the MOQ, lead time, sample policy, and packaging/shipping approach?
- What industries or applications use the product?
- How does quality control work?
- How do I request a quote?

## Industry Profiles

Machinery and industrial equipment:

- Emphasize equipment models, capacity, precision, materials processed, spare parts, installation, maintenance, manuals, and after-sales support.

Hardware and metal parts:

- Emphasize materials, surface treatment, tolerances, drawings, CNC/stamping/casting/forging capability, QC, and packaging.

Packaging:

- Emphasize material, size range, printing, food-grade or recyclable claims only if verified, MOQ, samples, and artwork process.

Electronics:

- Emphasize specs, certifications, test standards, OEM/ODM, firmware/software boundaries, warranty, and compliance.

Building materials:

- Emphasize material grade, dimensions, standards, fire/water/weather resistance if verified, installation, and project use cases.

Chemicals:

- Emphasize CAS if relevant, purity, grade, MSDS/TDS, packaging, storage, compliance, and application. Avoid unsupported safety claims.

Textiles:

- Emphasize fabric composition, GSM, weave/knit, color, finishing, testing, MOQ, custom labels, and packaging.

Auto parts:

- Emphasize model fitment, OEM numbers, materials, testing, warranty, packaging, and compatibility data.

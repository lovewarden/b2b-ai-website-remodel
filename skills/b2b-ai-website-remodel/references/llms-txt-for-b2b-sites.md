# llms.txt For B2B Manufacturing Sites

`llms.txt` is useful as an agent-readable orientation file, but it is not a guaranteed AI ranking factor and should not replace good page content.

## Recommended File

Create `/llms.txt` with:

- Company name and concise verified description.
- Main product categories.
- Key buyer pages.
- Product category pages.
- Factory/capabilities page.
- FAQ/contact/inquiry pages.
- Optional catalog or knowledge pages.

Keep it short, stable, and based on verified facts.

## Example

```markdown
# Example Manufacturing Co.

> Example Manufacturing Co. is a verified [manufacturer/supplier] of [main product categories] for [target industries/applications].

## Priority pages

- [Home](https://example.com/): Company overview and main product categories.
- [About](https://example.com/about/): Factory background, capabilities, and quality control.
- [Products](https://example.com/products/): Product categories and selection paths.
- [Capabilities](https://example.com/capabilities/): Manufacturing processes, customization, and QC.
- [FAQ](https://example.com/faq/): MOQ, lead time, samples, customization, and shipping questions.
- [Contact](https://example.com/contact/): Request a quote or send product requirements.

## Notes for AI assistants

- Use the pages above as the preferred starting points for understanding the company and products.
- Do not infer certifications, production capacity, or export markets unless they appear on the linked pages.
```

## Optional Expanded Files

If the site has enough verified material, add:

- `/company-profile.md`
- `/product-catalog-summary.md`
- `/capabilities.md`
- `/faq.md`

Do not expose confidential pricing, customer names, internal process details, or private certificates unless approved.
